About

I'm a PhD student in the School of Computer and Cyber Sciences at Augusta University, working as a Graduate Research Assistant under the mentorship of Dr. Shiwei Fang. My research sits at the intersection of Cyber-Physical Systems, human sensing, IoT privacy, and human–computer interaction.

I'm interested in how ubiquitous sensors and connected devices can understand human behavior and context — and how we design those systems so they remain private, trustworthy, and genuinely useful to the people they serve. My work blends systems building with human-centered evaluation: prototyping sensing pipelines, probing the privacy implications of always-on IoT devices, and studying how people actually interact with the smart environments we build for them.

Before starting my PhD, I completed an M.S. in Computer Science at Augusta University, where I also worked as a Graduate and Undergraduate Course Assistant, teaching Python programming and mentoring 70+ students. I did my undergraduate degree (B.Tech, Information Technology, specialization in Information Security) at Jaypee University of Information Technology in India. I've also spent time in industry as an IT Systems Analyst, building production web platforms with React/Next.js and Node.js/Express.
