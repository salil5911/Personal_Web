01 About

I'm a PhD student in the School of Computer and Cyber Sciences at Augusta University, where my work sits at the intersection of human sensing, IoT privacy, and human–computer interaction. I'm interested in how ubiquitous sensors and connected devices can understand human behavior and context — and how we design those systems so they remain private, trustworthy, and genuinely useful to the people they serve.

My research blends systems building with human-centered evaluation: prototyping sensing pipelines, probing the privacy implications of always-on IoT devices, and studying how people actually interact with the smart environments we build for them.

02 Research Focus

Human Sensing

Designing and evaluating sensing systems that infer human activity, physiology, and context from ambient and wearable signals.

IoT Privacy

Investigating privacy risks in connected devices and smart environments, and building mechanisms that give users real control over their data.

Human–Computer Interaction

Studying how people perceive, trust, and interact with intelligent systems — and translating those insights into better interface and system design.
