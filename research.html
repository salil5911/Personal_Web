Research

Human Sensing

Designing and evaluating sensing systems that infer human activity, physiology, and context from ambient and wearable signals, as part of ongoing Cyber-Physical Systems research.

IoT Privacy

Investigating privacy risks in connected devices and smart environments — from AR-based privacy visualizations to LLM-driven context fusion for user consent — and building mechanisms that give users real control over their data.

Human–Computer Interaction

Studying how people perceive, trust, and interact with intelligent systems — and translating those insights into better interface and system design, including secure multi-party computation and privacy-preserving analytics.
